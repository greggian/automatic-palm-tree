NWS NATIONAL HURRICANE CENTER MIAMI FL       EP042025
1800 UTC TUE JUL 15 2025

Dalila has weakened considerably since peak intensity on Saturday
as it has moved into an environment of increasing vertical wind
shear and markedly cooler sea surface temperatures.  The current
SSTs beneath the storm are around 24-25 degrees C, well below what
is needed to sustain tropical convection.  The convective
organization has decreased dramatically and the low-level
circulation is becoming more difficult to identify in satellite
imagery.

The track guidance is in good agreement, showing continued
west-northwestward motion over the next 24-36 hours.  Dalila should
degenerate into a remnant low by tomorrow evening as deep convection
associated with the circulation dissipates entirely.

No interests in the Eastern Pacific need to monitor Dalila at this
time.
