TROPICAL STORM FOUR-E FORECAST DISCUSSION NUMBER   1
NWS NATIONAL HURRICANE CENTER MIAMI FL       EP042025
1800 UTC WED JUL 09 2025

A vigorous area of convection associated with a broad surface low
pressure system has organized sufficiently over the past 12 hours
to be designated as Tropical Storm Four-E.  Satellite data indicate
that a low-level circulation has consolidated near 13.5N 98.5W.
Deep convection is well-organized with banding features developing
on the northern and western sides of the circulation.

FOUR-E is moving westward to west-northwestward and this general
motion is expected to continue for the next 72 hours or so as the
storm is steered by a subtropical ridge to the north.  After that
time, an approaching trough could cause the storm to curve more to
the north or northwest.

The intensity forecast calls for gradual strengthening during the
next few days as Four-E moves over warm sea surface temperatures
(29-30 degrees C) and in a low wind shear environment.  The system
could reach hurricane strength in about 48-60 hours.  Thereafter,
increasing shear and cooler SSTs are expected to induce weakening.
