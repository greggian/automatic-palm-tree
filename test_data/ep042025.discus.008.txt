1800 UTC SAT JUL 12 2025

Dalila has reached hurricane strength with maximum sustained winds
estimated at 80 kt based on objective satellite intensity estimates
(SATCON, ADT, DMINT) and a subjective analysis of the latest
satellite imagery.  Microwave imagery from earlier today shows a
well-defined warm core structure and deep convection surrounding
the circulation center.  A small eye feature is apparent in the
latest infrared imagery.

The storm continues on a westward to west-northwestward track,
steered by a deep-layer subtropical ridge.  Increasing vertical
wind shear associated with an approaching mid-latitude trough is
expected to cause steady weakening after 24 hours.  Sea surface
temperatures ahead of the storm are running 1-2 degrees below
normal for this time of year, which will also contribute to the
expected weakening trend.

The NHC track guidance is in reasonable agreement through 72 hours.
The models diverge more significantly at the longer time ranges as
the interaction with the trough becomes more important.  The NHC
forecast closely follows the multi-model consensus.
